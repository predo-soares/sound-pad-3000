# Privacy Policy — Sound Pad 3000

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Sound Pad 3000 (“the app”) is a local sound board for iPhone and iPad, published by Pedro Soares.

This policy describes what the app does with information on your device. It is the privacy policy for the App Store listing.

## Summary

The app does not require an account. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Sounds you play, import, or record stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

## Data the app uses

### Audio you add to pads

- **Imported files.** If you choose an audio file, the app copies it into its private storage on the device so the pad still works if you delete the original.
- **Recordings.** If you tap Record, iOS asks for microphone access. A recording is stored on the device and assigned to that pad only after you confirm. You can deny the microphone and still import a file instead.

These files are not sent off the device by the app.

### Microphone

The microphone is used only to record a custom sound for a pad. It is not used to listen in the background, and it is not used until you tap Record.

### Factory samples

The app may ship with short bundled sound clips (Mixkit, used under the Mixkit License). Those files are part of the app binary. They are not recordings of you.

### Device identifiers and usage analytics

The app does not collect IDFA, IDFV for advertising, analytics events, crash reports to a third party, or contact information.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos (the app uses the Files picker for audio, not the photo library), browsing history, or payment information.

## Third parties

The app does not share pad audio or microphone recordings with third parties. Apple may process information as described in Apple’s privacy policy when you download the app from the App Store or use TestFlight.

## Retention and deletion

Pad names and audio copies live in the app’s sandbox until you remove a sound from a pad, delete the app, or erase the device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and the new version will be published in this repository.
